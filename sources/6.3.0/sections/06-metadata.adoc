[[sec-metadata]]
== METADATA

[[sec-introduction-6]]
=== Introduction

<<bm-metadata,Metadata>> is fundamental to ensure that survey data is correctly understood and utilised as required for chart production or other purposes.  This Standard identifies the minimum <<bm-metadata,metadata>> that is to be provided with hydrographic surveys conducted for safety of navigation.  Where additional <<bm-metadata,metadata>> is available this should be included to enhance the value of the survey data for other uses.  Examples of <<bm-metadata,metadata>> are shown in paragraph 6.2. 

[[sec-metadata-content]]
=== Metadata Content

<<bm-metadata,Metadata>> can be provided in any format such as in the Report of Survey or embedded within a specific <<bm-metadata,metadata>> file.  The chosen format should support discovery, clarity of understanding, and software compatibility.  Each hydrographic office or authority may adopt <<bm-metadata,metadata>> requirements beyond that specified here and should develop and document a list of additional <<bm-metadata,metadata>> used for their survey data.  The table below should be seen as a schema, and not a final data model.
