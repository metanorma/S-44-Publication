[appendix]
== Guidance for A Priori and A Posteriori Quality Control

[[bm-annex-c-guidance]]
[NOTE]
====
*Note*: This annex is *not* an integral part of the S-44 Standards and will be removed when the information therein is fully incorporated into IHO Publication C-13, _Manual on Hydrography_.
====

The S-44 standards refer to quality standards for both _a priori_ and _a posteriori_ results.  In this guidance a brief view on how to determine the uncertainties for _a priori_ and _a posteriori_ is given.  Determining uncertainties is necessary for any technique used in hydrographic surveys.  Methods to establish the <<bm-uncertainty,uncertainty>> may differ greatly for each survey technique used.

A preliminary task is to check the capability of the total system, to ensure that it can meet the requirements for horizontal and vertical uncertainty, and <<bm-feature-detection,feature detection>> ability according to the specified order.  Well known reference areas should be used to prevent any vertical offset on measurements.  Qualification on these reference areas should be carried out periodically.

[[sec-c-1a-priori-uncertainty]]
=== C.1 _A Priori_ Uncertainty

The <<bm-a-priori-uncertainty,_a priori_ uncertainty>> is a predicted value based on best practise estimations of all factors affecting the measurements.   Uncertainties from each measurement instrument and from environmental influences contribute to the total uncertainty.  Calculating the total <<bm-uncertainty,uncertainty>> horizontally and vertically prior to the survey will affirm to the hydrographer that the survey requirements will be feasible with the selected equipment in the environment of the survey area.  If the survey requirements are not achieved, other equipment or survey techniques may be necessary for that particular environment.

Estimations of the equipment and environment uncertainties should be adjusted or assessed.  By this, the <<bm-a-priori-uncertainty,_a priori_ uncertainty>> is updated during the survey.

[[sec-c-2a-posteriori-uncertainty]]
=== C.2 _A Posteriori_ Uncertainty

Fundamentally the hydrographer is most interested in the <<bm-a-posteriori-uncertainty,_a posteriori_ uncertainty>>.

The measurements are the end result and contain all errors involved in the total process. While the most reliable <<bm-a-posteriori-uncertainty,_a posteriori_ uncertainty>> estimate will be obtained on a well-known reference area, the absence of such reference does not preclude the use of statistical calculations on the measurements in order to obtain <<bm-a-posteriori-uncertainty,_a posteriori_ uncertainty>> based on measurement repeatability.
